SHIPPING & RETURNS

Orders are printed, programmed and assembled in Texas. Typical production time is about 72 hours, and orders ship in under a week via USPS/UPS within the United States.

Shipping is a flat rate, and free above the threshold shown in your cart. Custom orders are quoted separately.

If your item arrives damaged or the NFC chip does not work, email us within 30 days and we will repair or replace it. Custom-designed items are made to order and are not returnable unless defective.

TERMS

By ordering you agree that product details, pricing and availability may change without notice, and that an order is accepted once we confirm it by email. NFC tags are programmed with the content you provide; you are responsible for that content and for having the right to use it.

PRIVACY

We only collect the information you send us (name, email, shipping address, order details) in order to fulfil and support your order. We do not sell your information. Your cart is stored in your own browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, we store your name, email, saved carts, order requests and last-used shipping details in your account (hosted on Google Firebase) so you can see them again; we never see your Google password. Email us to have your account data deleted.
